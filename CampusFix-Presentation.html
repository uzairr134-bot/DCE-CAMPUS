How it works

A simple four-step workflow

01 · ReportStudents add a title, location, category, priority, description, and optional photo.
02 · NotifyUrgent reports can trigger an administrator buzzer and browser notification.
03 · RespondAdministrators review reports, assign progress, and share a resolution photo.
04 · ConfirmThe reporting student reviews the evidence and taps Mark as resolved.
